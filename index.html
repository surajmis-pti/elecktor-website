Inside the plant

Our Factory

A plant in Sonipat with copper bunching, PVC extrusion lines, plug moulding machines and a dedicated assembly and testing floor.

How a cord is made

From copper to carton

Every stage runs under one roof, so quality, length and packing stay consistent from batch to batch.

Copper pay-off

Fine copper strands are fed from bobbin racks with controlled tension.

Bunching

Strands are bunched into flexible conductors for each core.

PVC extrusion

Insulation and outer sheath are extruded on dual-extruder lines.

Coiling & cutting

Cable is coiled and cut to 2 m, 2.5 yard or 3 m lengths.

Plug moulding

2 pin and 3 pin plugs are moulded directly onto the cord.

Testing & packing

Cords are checked and packed one per branded pouch, then into sacks.

Buyer questions

FAQ

Quick answers for dealers and OEM buyers. Anything else, WhatsApp us.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is one sack per product: 400 pieces for 3 pin cords and 600 pieces for 2 pin cords. Quantities go up in pouches of 10 pieces.

Are the prices on the website final?

Website prices include 18% GST and are a guide. Power cord rates move with copper, so our team confirms the final rate when we accept your order.

What are the payment and freight terms?

Payment is 100% advance against our proforma invoice. Freight is on the buyer's account; we dispatch through your preferred transporter.

Do you make custom lengths or OEM cords?

Yes. For custom lengths, colours or OEM packing, send an enquiry with your quantity and we will share a quote.

How do I track my order?

After you place an order you get an Order ID (for example ELK-1001). Use "Track Order" with that ID and your mobile number to see the latest status.
